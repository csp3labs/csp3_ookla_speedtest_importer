How to Export & Save Results from Ookla Speedtest App

  1. Open the official Ookla Speedtest mobile app.
  2. Navigate to your test History tab.
  3. Tap the export or share option to generate/save your test logs as a results.csv file.
  4. Save or upload your exported results.csv directly to Google Drive or OneDrive (Onebox) for easy retrieval.
  5. Source tagging will be set to Ookla Speedtest CSV Exporter for items exported via this tool.
  6. Only 1 unique test ID is recorded in the Google Sheet. Duplicates will be dropped.
  7. Exporter only considers LTE and NR test results. WIFI tests are excluded on the export.

Name is required before you can process and export results.
